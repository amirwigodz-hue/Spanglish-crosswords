At Spanglish Crosswords, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This privacy policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by our website and how it is used.

1. Google AdSense and DoubleClick Cookie

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

2. Information We Collect

Analytics: We use Google Analytics to monitor and analyze web traffic. Google Analytics is a web analytics service offered by Google that tracks and reports website traffic. This data is shared with other Google services. Google may use the collected data to contextualize and personalize the ads of its own advertising network.

Feedback Form: If you use our feedback form, we collect the contents of your message and your email address (if provided). This information is processed securely through Formspree and is used solely to respond to your inquiries and improve the website. We do not sell or share this personal information with third parties.

3. Log Files

Like many other websites, Spanglish Crosswords makes use of log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, type of browser, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends, administer the site, track user's movement around the site, and gather demographic information. IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Consent
