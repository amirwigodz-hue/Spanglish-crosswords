About Spanglish Crosswords

Welcome to Spanglish Crosswords, a dedicated space for language learners and puzzle enthusiasts to bridge the gap between English and Spanish. The goal of this site is simple: to make expanding your vocabulary an engaging, interactive experience rather than a chore.

The Story Behind the Puzzles

As I started building my own foundational knowledge of Spanish, I realized that language learning requires a different kind of analytical thinking—one that benefits heavily from context, repetition, and play.

To combine my background in coding with my language goals, I wrote a custom Python script that mathematically constructs these bilingual arroword grids directly from curated vocabulary word banks. The result is this growing collection of crosswords designed to seamlessly test translation skills in both directions.

Built for Everyday Practice

Whether you are a native Spanish speaker mastering English or an English speaker picking up Spanish, these puzzles focus on practical, everyday vocabulary. The site is entirely custom-coded to ensure smooth, mobile-friendly gameplay. With features like automatic cursor progression, you can easily solve a quick puzzle on your phone during a commute or a break.

Enjoy the puzzles, and good luck with your language journey!
